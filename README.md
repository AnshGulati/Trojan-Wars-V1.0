## 🚀 Overview
Trojan Wars is an intense 2D top-down shooter game where players take on the role of a skilled Gun-Man infiltrating an enemy hideout and secret control center. Armed with just a pistol at the start, players must dodge, shoot, strategize, and survive through hostile environments filled with dangerous enemies and hidden threats. The objective is to defeat waves of intelligent enemy AI, collect vital keys, upgrade your arsenal, and ultimately face a powerful turret boss guarding the enemy’s control room.
With immersive gameplay, fast-paced action, custom sound design, and strategic mechanics like inventory management and weapon switching, Trojan Wars delivers a compelling and action-packed shooter experience.

## ❓ Problem Statement
Many 2D shooters fail to combine intelligent enemy behavior, real-time strategy, and immersive environmental design in a single package. Trojan Wars addresses this by integrating smart A* pathfinding-based enemy AI, a key-based progression system, and diverse player mechanics—making each level a test of reflexes, resource management, and tactical planning.

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Provide immersive and strategic 2D shooter gameplay through intelligent enemy encounters and weapon variety.
- Implement level-based progression using keys, health pickups, and weapon management.
- Deliver a dynamic player experience with responsive controls, fast reflex challenges, and boss fights.
- Optimize AI behavior using A* pathfinding and ensure fluid player-enemy interactions.

## 🛠 Technologies Used
- **Engine**: Unity
- **Programming Language**: C#
- **AI**: A* Pathfinding Package
- **Design**: Custom 2D assets, animation, sound effects
- **UI**: Interactive game UI with inventory and HUD elements

## 🧠 Features & Capabilities
- **Top-Down View**: Offers full control over movement and strategy.
- **Shooting Mechanics**: Start with a pistol; shoot in any direction with limited ammo.
- **Reloading**: Reloading adds vulnerability and timing strategy.
- **Enemy AI**: Enemies use A* pathfinding to track and attack the player dynamically.
- **Weapon Pickups**: Collect stronger weapons (Shotgun, Machine Gun) with better stats.
- **Dodging**: Actively avoid incoming bullets while maintaining offense.
- **Level Progression**: Navigate through increasingly difficult levels with keys required to unlock doors.
- **Boss Fight**: Final turret-based boss requires strategy and firepower to defeat.
- **Inventory System**: Store and switch between weapons, keys, and items in a custom player inventory.
- **Health System**: Health bar depletes upon taking damage; health pickups available throughout the level.
- **Game-Over State**: Losing all health or falling to enemies ends the run.

## 🌍 Scope of the Project
This 2D game project aims to:

- **Deliver High-Intensity Combat**: Combine shooting precision, reflex-driven movement, and enemy AI tactics for a challenging experience.
- **Build Strategic Depth**: Offer inventory management, limited ammo, reloading, and different weapon types to encourage planning and adaptation.
- **Demonstrate Core Game Design Concepts**: Showcase knowledge in Unity development, AI programming, level design, and visual storytelling through a polished top-down shooter game.
